## 2. Verdict

**PASS WITH FIXES.** The area is unusually thorough: the MCP surface is genuinely narrow (six tools, forbidden-field detection, separate listeners per credential class), the worker/harness split (ADR-0402) is sound, atomic claims are correctly delegated to plan 29's `executions_one_active` index + `FOR UPDATE`/`SKIP LOCKED`, L-1 and L-2 are mitigated point by point with re-verification ACs, and the fake-scenario table covers every SPEC §103 name. No finding makes the system unsafe on its own *given* the downstream layers (CI identity checks, PR-head status invalidation, human merge). But several crash/restart, trust-anchor and DAG/AC problems would make a literal implementation of plan 26 wrong, and must be fixed before C01/C02/C08 start.

- **C08 ↔ C10:** 24-AC-12 (GATE-C08) requires the C10 setup and a RED_VERIFIED task; C10 depends on C08. This is a real cycle.
- **C01 ↔ C03:** 26-AC-11 (GATE-C01) needs Forgejo base-ref resolution and base-revision reads, which are C03 mechanics; C03 depends on C01.
- **C02 missing C03, C07:** GATE-C02 needs deploy keys, compare and context compilation. These are missing edges, not a cycle. C01 also uses Alertmanager (26-AC-17) without depending on P09.

**Runtime and bootstrap ordering:** no deadlock found.
- Runner readiness needs `sc-pg`, migrations and policy only. It does not need the Gateway, Hermes, Forgejo or Woodpecker. Policy-commit resolution and webhook verification degrade without blocking readiness.
- The Gateway's readiness needs the Runner's ledger migrations (CD-04), a one-way dependency.
- Hermes needs the Runner to render `hermes-managed-env` (one-way, bounded by the 10-min preflight wait plus restart). It also needs the internal registry pull secret, which is missing (RA-9).
- The Runner and `sc-ci-config` call each other (intents and served attestations), and the Runner and Forgejo call each other (API and webhooks). Both pairs degrade safely: no intent means no trusted config, and the 5-min resync covers missed webhooks. Neither is a bootstrap cycle, because P08/`sc-ci-config` exist before C01 and must serve non-agent pipelines without the Runner (plan 20 should state this explicitly).
- The heavy-slot dispatch gate polls the Woodpecker queue API. Whether the gate opens or stays closed when Woodpecker is unreachable is undefined. Recommend fail-closed with a metric and an alert (add to plan 26 §15).
- In black start, the Runner/Gateway/Hermes-preflight images come from the Forgejo OCI registry, which is restored before the `strange-company` layer. The order is consistent.
